Personal Statement

The first time I disassembled a computer at the age of twelve, I did not see a machine; I saw a universe of logic waiting to be explored. That curiosity has since evolved into a disciplined pursuit of computer science, culminating in my decision to apply to the Master of Science in Computer Science program at Carnegie Mellon University.

During my undergraduate preparation at Beijing 21st Century International School, I built a solid foundation in mathematics and physics, achieving A* in A-Level Mathematics and A in Physics. Beyond the classroom, I led our school's programming club, where I organized weekly algorithm workshops and guided a twelve-member team to develop a campus scheduling application now serving over 2,000 students and faculty members.

My research experience at Beijing Academy of Artificial Intelligence further sharpened my academic vision. Working as a research assistant, I contributed to the construction of a multimodal data annotation system and independently completed quality inspection for over 30,000 samples. This experience taught me that rigorous engineering and creative research are two sides of the same coin.

Carnegie Mellon University's emphasis on interdisciplinary innovation, particularly the intersection of machine learning and systems, aligns precisely with my academic goals. I am eager to contribute to and learn from CMU's vibrant research community, and to lay a lasting foundation for my future career in artificial intelligence.
